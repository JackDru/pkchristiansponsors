Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

PK Christian Sponsors ("we," "us," "our") respects your privacy. This page explains what information we collect when you use this website, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, such as when you sponsor a child, make a donation, sign up for our newsletter or prayer team, or contact us. This may include your name, email address, mailing address, and phone number.

We do not collect or store your credit card or bank information. Donations and sponsorships are processed by Stripe, a PCI-compliant payment processor, through their own secure checkout page. We never see or store your full payment details.

Like most websites, we automatically collect some technical information — such as browser type, device type, and pages visited — through standard web server and analytics logs, to help us understand how the site is used and to keep it secure.

How We Use Your Information

  • To process your sponsorship or donation and send you receipts
  • To send you updates about the child or program you support, including periodic video updates
  • To respond to questions you send us and provide donor support
  • To send newsletters or prayer requests you've opted into, which you can unsubscribe from at any time
  • To improve this website and our communications

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for their marketing purposes.

Sharing Information

We share information only with service providers who help us operate — such as Stripe for payment processing and our email and forms providers — and only to the extent needed for them to perform that service. We may also disclose information if required by law.

Children's Privacy

This website is intended for adult donors and sponsors. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children through this site. Information about sponsored children in Pakistan is collected and safeguarded by Grace Charity School under its own child-protection policies, and is shared with sponsors only in the limited, age-appropriate way needed to maintain the sponsor relationship.

Data Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect the information you share with us. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, but we work to protect your information appropriately.

Your Choices

You can unsubscribe from any email communication at any time using the link in that email, or by contacting us directly. You may also request that we correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, subject to our legal and recordkeeping obligations as a nonprofit organization.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We'll post any changes on this page with a new "last updated" date.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, email us at pkchristiansponsors@gmail.com.
