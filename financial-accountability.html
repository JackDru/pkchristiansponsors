Financial Accountability

You trusted us with your gift. Here's exactly how we steward it — and how you can check our work.

Where Your Gift Goes

95 Cents of Every Dollar Reaches the Work

We keep overhead low on purpose. Almost everything you give goes directly to Grace Charity School's students, teachers, and building — not to running PK Christian Sponsors.

Programs & Direct Aid95%
Administration5%

Every $40 Sponsorship, Itemized

We don't ask you to fund a vague "mission." A full $40/month sponsorship maps to specific, named needs for one child at Grace Charity School:

  • Tuition & Fees$13
  • Meals$7
  • Housing$6
  • Books & Supplies$5
  • Clean Water$4
  • Security & Child Protection$3
  • Mentoring & Video Updates$2

Nonprofit Status

PK Christian Sponsors is a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization. Donations made to PK Christian Sponsors are tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law. You'll receive a receipt by email for every gift.

Annual Financial Report

Once a year, we publish a summary of our income, expenses, and program spending so donors can see exactly where the money went — not just where we say it went. Our current-year report is available on request.

Want a copy, or want to ask a specific question about how your gift was used? Email us at pkchristiansponsors@gmail.com and we'll send it directly. We would rather answer the question than have you wonder.

Break the Cycle

We fund Grace Charity School's fight to free children from brick-kiln slavery in Pakistan — 700+ freed, one sponsor at a time.
