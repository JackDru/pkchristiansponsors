Right Now, Today

A Specific Child Is Waiting on a Sponsor

You already know why this matters. Here's what's true right now: there's a specific child at Grace Charity School whose place depends on funding that isn't there yet. Your sponsorship covers their tuition, meals, housing, books, water, security, and mentoring — starting the month you give, not someday.

Every month that goes unfunded is a month closer to that child going back to the kiln instead of staying in the classroom. Every six months, you'll receive a video of your sponsored child saying your name and thanking you, by name, for your gift — proof it's making a difference, because you acted when it counted.

Why Grace Charity School

Christians in Pakistan Are Targeted For This

Christians make up less than 2% of Pakistan's population, yet religious minorities account for as much as half of all bonded laborers in the brick-kiln regions of Punjab and Sindh — locked out of other work by prejudice, then trapped by debt. Grace Charity School exists specifically to break that cycle for the children caught in it.

Its founder, Pastor Victor Sammuel, was abducted and held for 24 days in 2012 because of this work. He didn't stop.
