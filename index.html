Our Mission

Every Child Deserves to Know They Are Loved

PK Christian Sponsors exists to release children from poverty in Jesus' name. Through the generosity of sponsors like you, we walk alongside local churches and communities to provide what every child needs to thrive — physically, academically, socially, and spiritually.

Since our founding, we've grown from a single congregation's outreach into a global network of ministry partners, all committed to the same conviction: lasting change happens when the love of Christ meets practical, everyday care.
