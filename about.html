Our Story

From One Congregation to a Global Ministry

PK Christian Sponsors began in 2005 when a small congregation felt called to support a handful of children in a neighboring community facing extreme poverty. What started as a handful of sponsorships grew, through the faithfulness of ordinary families, into a ministry now serving tens of thousands of children across 34 countries.

We remain committed to our founding conviction: that lasting transformation happens through long-term relationship, local church partnership, and the good news of Jesus Christ — not through short-term aid alone.

Our Ministry Partner

Grace Charity School, Pakistan

PK Christian Sponsors is the U.S.-based advocacy and fundraising partner for Grace Charity School — we don't operate the school ourselves, but walk alongside its on-the-ground team, channeling sponsor and donor support directly to their work.

Located in the heart of Pakistan, Grace Charity School is a beacon of hope for children who have faced unimaginable challenges. Through education, mentorship, and faith, the school is breaking the cycle of poverty and offering these children a chance to dream again.

Grace Charity's Work

Serving Street and Brick Kiln Children

Grace Charity is committed to serving street and brick kiln children in Pakistan. The organization provides education, support, and advocacy to help these children break free from the cycle of poverty and exploitation — because every child deserves a chance to receive an education, grow in a safe environment, and reach their full potential.

Through its programs, Grace Charity works to:

  • Provide education and vocational training to street and brick kiln children
  • Support families and communities in breaking the cycle of poverty
  • Advocate for children's rights and work toward the elimination of child labor
  • Offer a safe, nurturing environment where children can grow and thrive

By supporting PK Christian Sponsors, you help sustain this work and make a real difference in the lives of these vulnerable children.

Boots on the Ground

Pastor Victor Sammuel

Founder & Principal, Grace Charity School

Pastor Victor leads Grace Charity School on the ground in Pakistan. Raised in a large family that knew real poverty, he came to faith in 2001 and was baptized soon after — the start of a calling that reshaped the rest of his life. By 2007 he was preaching in the open streets of his community, and what began as informal gatherings grew into the ministry now known as God's Grace Ministries of Pakistan.

As he worked alongside families in some of the country's poorest brick-kiln communities, he felt called to start a school for the children working there — with no funding, no building, and no guarantee it would work. In 2012, that conviction was tested: he was abducted and held for 24 days before being freed. He didn't stop. He married Sofia in 2013, and by 2016 the ministry had its own school building. Today, Grace Charity School serves hundreds of children — proof, in his words, that God provides even when the odds say otherwise.

Mission & Vision

Our vision is a world where every child, family, and community has the opportunity to experience the fullness of life found in Christ — free from the effects of poverty, spiritually rooted, and equipped to become the person God created them to be.

Statement of Faith

What We Believe

PK Christian Sponsors affirms the historic Christian faith: one God eternally existing in three persons; the Bible as God's inspired Word; salvation through faith in Jesus Christ alone; and the call of every believer to love God and love neighbor — including "the least of these" (Matthew 25:40).

Every program we run is offered without regard to a family's religion, and participation in any spiritual activity is always voluntary.
