Ministry That Meets the Whole Person

Every program is delivered in partnership with local churches, built for lasting change — not short-term relief.

Core Program

Child Sponsorship

At the heart of our ministry is one-to-one sponsorship: a relationship between a sponsor and a child, sustained through monthly giving, letters, and prayer. Sponsorship funds education, nutritious meals, medical checkups, and discipleship activities tailored to each child's community.

Sponsors and children exchange letters throughout the year, and many sponsors choose to visit their sponsored child through organized trips.

Education & Discipleship

Growing in Knowledge and Faith

We provide school fees, uniforms, and supplies, along with tutoring for children who have fallen behind. Alongside academics, weekly Bible clubs and mentoring help children grow spiritually in a safe, encouraging environment.

  • After-school tutoring and literacy support
  • School fee and supply assistance
  • Age-appropriate Bible clubs and mentoring
  • Leadership development for teens
Clean Water & Health

Protecting Children From Preventable Illness

Access to clean water and basic healthcare remains out of reach for many of the families we serve. We partner with communities to drill wells, install water filtration, run mobile medical clinics, and provide nutritional support for malnourished children.

Community Development

Empowering Parents With Dignity

Long-term change happens when families can provide for themselves. Our community development programs offer vocational training, micro-loans, and agricultural support so parents can build sustainable livelihoods for their households.
